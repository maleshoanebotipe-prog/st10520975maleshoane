About Happy Place

Welcome to Happy Place, a Dealesville food business focused on delicious homemade meals and treats.

Our Story

Happy Place was started in 2000 by Nungu Nzondo in her home kitchen, where she baked scones using a family recipe passed down from her grandmother. In early 2026 we expanded into our own storefront.

We believe that good food should be comforting, affordable and made with care. Every order is prepared with attention to quality and customer satisfaction, whether you enjoy it at home, at work or on a special occasion.

Our Mission

To bring joy to everyone through delicious, home-cooked meals, with fresh ingredients and friendly service.

Why Choose Happy Place?
